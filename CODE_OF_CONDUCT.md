# Code of Conduct

CleanSweep contributors are expected to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment.

Be considerate in technical disagreement, focus criticism on code and behavior, welcome contributors of different experience levels, and do not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, or publication of private information. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled proportionately.
